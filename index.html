ReadStar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026
ReadStar is a reading app designed for children ages 3–6. We take your child's privacy seriously. Learning progress is stored on your device. Reading practice uses Apple speech recognition, which may process audio on Apple servers. Sharing is optional and controlled by a grown-up.

Who we are

App name: ReadStar

Developer: Ronnie Reyes

Contact: Ronnie.Reyes@live.com

Who this app is for

ReadStar is designed for children ages 3–6 . A grown-up controls settings, purchases, and sharing.

How we use the microphone

ReadStar uses the microphone with your permission during reading practice. The optional parent voice studio also lets a grown-up record word pronunciations for playback.

Reading practice uses Apple's Speech framework. Apple may process speech on its servers under its speech-recognition permissions and terms. ReadStar does not save reading-practice audio. Optional voice-studio recordings are saved locally until removed. Speech recognition may make mistakes and does not certify independent reading ability.

What we store on the device

ReadStar stores your child's learning progress locally on the device only. This includes:

  • Which words have been mastered
  • Stars earned during practice
  • Personalized storybooks generated for your child
  • A few simple app preferences (e.g., whether illustrations are shown)

Progress and preferences use local storage. ReadStar does not operate a progress-sync server. Device backups may include local app data according to your Apple backup settings. A grown-up can choose to export a reading certificate with a nickname approved for that individual share.

Personalized storybooks and sharing

New stories are assembled on the device from the bundled story library and mastered word texts. Previously saved books are retained. This version does not call Anthropic or another external story-generation service.

Certificates include the book title and practice results. A grown-up must pass the parent gate and approve any optional nickname for each export. Certificates never include voice recordings or photos automatically. Information you choose to share is handled by the destination app or service.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information from children or adults.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not show advertisements of any kind.
  • We do not sell personal data. Apple speech processing and optional parent-initiated sharing are described above.
  • We do not have accounts, logins, or social features.
  • We do not allow public sharing or user-generated content visible to other users.

In-app purchases

ReadStar offers a free trial with the first 20 words and one personalized storybook. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks the full 140-word curriculum and all storybooks. The purchase button is gated behind a parent confirmation (parental gate) so children cannot purchase anything on their own. There are no subscriptions or recurring charges.

Parental controls

Parents can reset all of their child's stored data at any time from the Parent Settings screen inside the app. Tap the gear icon, solve the parent gate question, and choose "Reset All Progress."
